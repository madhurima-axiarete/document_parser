**NOTE:** Elastosis perforans serpiginosa presents as small papules, either grouped or in a circular or serpiginous arrangement, often on the face, neck, upper extremities, and trunk. The disorder has a predilection for males and typically occurs in the second decade. Up to a third of cases have been reported to be associated with an associated systemic disorder or connective tissue disorder. Some cases are associated with prolonged use of penicillamine. Clinical correlation is recommended. <!-- text, from page 0 (l=0.05,t=0.81,r=0.98,b=0.93) -->
