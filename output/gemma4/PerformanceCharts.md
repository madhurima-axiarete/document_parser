## Measuring Our Climate Impact

We have established a global carbon accounting system in accordance with the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard, and we independently verify our emissions against the ISO 14064-3 standard. We report emissions in metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent ($\text{tCO}_2\text{e}$).

Relative to our 2018 baseline, our 2022 emissions represent a 30% lower in absolute terms and 54% lower on a per-person basis. In comparison with our absolute emissions to 2021, our emissions in 2022 decreased, due mainly to the return of business travel in 2022 following the easing of COVID-19 travel restrictions.
